What's Inside Project 23

CalendarProductivity
Import your iPhone `.ics` calendar, customize event colors, manage recurring schedules, and view daily agendas.
To-do ListProductivity
Bring in your iPhone Reminders and organize tasks with due dates, priority flags, categories, and custom tags.

Book a focus flight by destination or target flight time, pick a cabin seat, and fly with live telemetry, map, and airplane window views.
NotesProductivity
Write and format rich documents with headings, checklists, callout blocks, links, highlights, and pinned notes.

Count down to the milestones and dates that matter most, with live second-by-second timers and calendar event linking.
WeatherUtility

Progressive Web App

Install on iPhone

  • 1
    Open in Safari (iOS) or Chrome/Edge
    Project 23 includes a native Web App Manifest and Apple touch icons for full-screen standalone mode.
  • 2
    Tap Share → "Add to Home Screen"
    Launches directly from your iPhone Home Screen without browser.
  • 3
    Sign In with Google for Cloud Sync
    Your events, reminders, notes, countdowns, and flight logs sync automatically across all your signed-in devices.
