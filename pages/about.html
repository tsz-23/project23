Install on iPhone

  • 1
    Open in Safari (iOS) or Chrome/Edge
    Project 23 includes a native Web App Manifest and Apple touch icons for full-screen standalone mode.
  • 2
    Tap Share → "Add to Home Screen"
    Launches directly from your iPhone Home Screen without browser toolbars.
  • 3
    Sign In with Google for Cloud Sync
    Your events, reminders, notes, vinyl records, watchlists, and PICO chats sync automatically across all your devices.
